LEVEL 1 IS MARKET MAKER. THE TUTORIAL ROUND IS THE MARKET FORCES LAB.

Level 1 asks whether you know the idea. This one asks whether you can use it on something messy. Every round is a brief drawn from what is actually happening right now — model pricing, export controls, a reunion tour, a grid under strain — and three linked steps that build on each other. You also stake how sure you are before each answer, which turns out to be the hardest part.

The unit is a caseFour briefs per run, three linked steps each. Step two assumes you understood step one.
ConvictionStake Low ×0.6, Medium ×1.0 or High ×1.8 before you answer. High pays nearly double — and costs you if you are wrong.
Clean sweepAll three steps of a brief correct earns a +250 chain bonus. Sustained reasoning, not lucky guesses.
No speed bonusUnlike Level 1, rushing earns you nothing. The clock is only a limit. Think.
Ambiguous is an answerSome cases genuinely cannot be signed. Saying so is correct, not a hedge — and calling it confidently pays.
Nothing is collectedNo sign-in, no roll number, nothing is submitted anywhere. Progress lives in this browser only.
