Market Maker

Twelve questions. Three tiers. A clock on every one of them and a streak multiplier that rewards not breaking. It covers the whole pre-midsemester arc — demand and supply, elasticity, surplus, price controls, tax incidence, opportunity cost — and it is designed to be replayed, because the questions rotate and the explanations are the point.

ScoringTier 1 100, Tier 2 200, Tier 3 350 base points. Answer faster, keep more.
Speed bonusUp to +50% of the base, decaying as the clock runs down.
Streak3 in a row ×1.2 · 5 ×1.5 · 8 ×2. One wrong answer resets it to zero.
No eliminationA wrong answer costs you points, never the run. Everybody finishes all twelve.
DiagnosticsYour accuracy is tracked per concept, so you can see which idea is leaking points.
GradesNone. This carries no marks and affects nothing on your transcript.

Be clear about what this is

This page is public and there is no login. Typing a roll number identifies you to your instructor; it does not verify you, and nothing here checks that the ID you enter is really yours. Treat it as a name tag, not a password.

What happens to your data:

  • Your scores, streaks and per-concept accuracy live only in this browser. Clear your site data and they are gone.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere while you play.
  • At the end of a run, you decide whether to press Submit. Only then does anything leave your device.
  • If you submit, your instructor receives the ID and display name you entered, your score, how many you got right, your longest streak, and which concepts you lost points on. No other student sees any of it.
